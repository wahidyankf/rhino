# Ideas

An idea is a two-pager, not a delivery plan. It exists to make a proposal arguable before anyone spends a week on it: the problem, the evidence, a sketch of a direction, and what would have to be true to promote it.

Keep each idea in one kebab-case Markdown file under the quadrant its evidence supports. Read this index before adding one, and consolidate overlapping briefs rather than accumulating near-duplicates.

## Shape

In order: an H1 title with a one-line summary and a dated provenance note; the problem and its evidence; why now; prior art with direct links and access dates; the proposed direction as a sketch; rough scope and non-goals; risks and open questions; and what success looks like with the signal that would promote it.

Keep file-level design, Gherkin, and delivery checklists out. A brief that already contains them is a plan that skipped its own review. When the important unknowns are resolved, replace the brief with a formal plan folder under [`../backlog/`](../backlog/README.md), carrying its evidence and decisions forward.

## Quadrants

Urgency and importance are judged from dated evidence, not from how the work feels. An idea with no evidence for either axis belongs in Q4 until it has some.

## Directory Map

- [Q1 — urgent and important](q1-urgent-important/README.md) indexes work with an immediate stake and real impact.
- [Q2 — important, not urgent](q2-not-urgent-important/README.md) indexes valuable work under no time pressure.
- [Q3 — urgent, not important](q3-urgent-not-important/README.md) indexes time-sensitive work with limited impact.
- [Q4 — neither urgent nor important](q4-not-urgent-not-important/README.md) indexes deliberately parked ideas.
